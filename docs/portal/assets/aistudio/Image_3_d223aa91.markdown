## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, clinically validated maternal healthcare experience tailored to the Egyptian and broader MENA market. It balances post-operative surgical precision with maternal empathy, removing the anxiety and clinical coldness typical of hospital recovery products. The aesthetic avoids mass-market pharmaceutical clutter in favor of an editorial, high-end therapeutic sanctuary.

### Design Movement & Mood
- **Style Blend:** Modern Clinical Editorial with Tonal Tactility. Clean, airy surfaces infused with warm undertones, soft tinted borders, and high-legibility Arabic typesetting.
- **Brand Personality:** Authoritative, reassuring, empathetic, uncompromisingly safe, and luxurious.
- **Emotional Arc:** Transitions the new mother from post-surgical vulnerability and apprehension (fear of infection, water exposure, scar disfigurement) into dignified, confident healing.
- **Conversion Philosophy:** Frictionless medical commerce driven by immediate credibility. Every viewport integrates explicit clinical validation (dermatologist/OB-GYN approvals, surgical-grade material stamps, sterile seal indicators) paired with high-conversion purchase ergonomics designed for postpartum convenience.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in medical authority and biological vitality, avoiding artificial starkness:

- **Primary (`#064E3B` - Deep Surgical Teal):** The anchor of trust and authority. Applied to primary calls to action, master clinical endorsements, and primary headline treatments.
- **Secondary (`#059669` - Vital Emerald):** Represents regeneration and cellular recovery. Used for active indicators, secondary actions, pricing emphasis, and validated status states.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Clinical Amber):** Applied sparingly to draw high-intent focus. Used for clinical alerts, protocol milestone markers, priority trust badges (e.g., "OB-GYN Approved"), and high-conversion urgency elements.
- **Success & Healing Accent (`#10B981` - Mint Solution):** Denotes sterile security, waterproof seal confirmation, and positive medical indicators.
- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#FBFDFB` (Medical Pearl) – soft on postpartum eyes, preventing blue-light fatigue.
  - Secondary Canvas: `#F3F7F5` (Tonal Alabaster) – divides narrative stages and technical protocol grids.
  - Border Tone: `#E2ECE6` (Sterile Slate) – ultra-subtle, keeping components structured without harsh delineations.

## Typography

The design system uses IBM Plex Sans Arabic as its typographic backbone. The typeface offers an ideal balance between technical engineering and humanistic Naskh stroke balance, providing clarity for Arabic medical instructions and high conversion copy.

### RTL & Typographic Rules
- **Line Heights:** Arabic typography requires 25% to 35% more vertical line height than Latin counterparts to prevent diacritics and ligatures from clashing. Line heights are calibrated accordingly.
- **Weight Pairing:** Body text remains strictly at weight `400` for effortless legibility. Boldness (`600`, `700`) is reserved for clinical benefits, protocol milestones, and conversion CTAs.
- **Numbers:** Always prioritize Eastern Arabic or standard Western numerals contextually, with tabular lining figures applied across all order summaries, pricing arrays, and protocol stages.
- **No Letter Spacing:** Negative or positive tracking must never be applied to Arabic letterforms, as it severs cursive glyph ligatures.

## Layout & Spacing

### RTL Layout Philosophy
The canvas is built exclusively around right-to-left optical flow (`dir="rtl"`). Reading vectors begin at the top right (brand credential, trust seals) and move downward toward action nodes on the bottom left (CTAs, buy boxes, protocol checkouts).

### Grid Infrastructure
- **Mobile (0 - 640px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Single-column stack with sticky bottom conversion bar.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Two-column split for clinical breakdown vs. cart actions.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Asymmetric splits (e.g., 7-column clinical proof, 5-column conversion box).

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing is generous to preserve a sense of calm and hygiene. Technical data (wound dimensions, silicone membrane thickness) is presented in structured grids buffered by `space-md`, while major narrative blocks breathe with `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy drop shadows in favor of tonal diffusion and light-bleed boundaries, maintaining an atmosphere of clean-room sterility and luxury.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FBFDFB` and tonal containers (`#F3F7F5`). No shadow. Outlined with `1px solid #E2ECE6`.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Product Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a diffused emerald-tinted ambient elevation: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.02)`. Border: `1px solid #E2ECE6`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Conversion Rails / Modals):** Enhanced ambient lift: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Mobile Sticky Checkout Bar):** Anchored to bottom edge with top-directed blur: `0 -4px 24px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.06)`, separated by a crisp top border (`#E2ECE6`).

## Shapes

The roundedness strategy uses a balanced radius (`roundedness: 2`) to evoke the soft, protective qualities of silicone scar sheets and medical hydrogels, avoiding both aggressive sharpness and juvenile roundness.

### Curvature Assignments
- **Primary Buttons & Form Inputs:** `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers tactile precision and confident tap targets.
- **Protocol Badges & Clinical Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to echo medical capsule geometry and dermal contouring.
- **Cards, Bundles & Step Guides:** `1rem` (16px). Creates cohesive, welcoming product modules.
- **Surgical Imagery & Testimonial Frames:** `1.5rem` (24px) with subtle internal clipping for an editorial finish.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion Actions
- **Primary Purchase CTA:** Solid Deep Surgical Teal (`#064E3B`) with high-contrast White text (`#FFFFFF`). Padding: `16px 32px`. Height: `54px`. Min-width: `100%` on mobile. Active states apply a subtle inner shade; focus rings display an accessible 2px offset in `#059669`.
- **Secondary / Protocol Guide CTA:** Crisp white surface with 1.5px border in `#064E3B`, text in `#064E3B`. Hover shifts to `#F3F7F5`.
- **Urgency Checkout Button:** Warm Amber (`#D97706`) background, white typography, used exclusively for direct "Buy Protocol Kit" actions.

### Clinical & Trust Badges
- **Dermatologist / OB-GYN Seals:** Pill containers using `#F3F7F5` with a `#059669` left-aligned indicator dot (in RTL, placed on the right). Micro-typography (`label-sm`) detailing hospital-grade sterilization standards (e.g., "معقم بالكامل - آمن للجراحة القيصرية").
- **Waterproof Shield Badge:** Mint tint surface (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) with deep emerald iconography illustrating the dual-layer barrier for showering.

### Product & Protocol Cards
- Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px `#E2ECE6` border and Level 1 elevation.
- Clear structural division: Visual demonstration on top, clinical phase (e.g., "الأسبوع 1 - 2: الحماية المائية") as a pill tag, recovery benefit highlights, and a localized price block with clear currency notation (e.g., "ج.م").

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2ECE6`. Radius: `0.5rem`.
- Text aligned strictly right with comfortable right-padding for contextual medical/shipping icons.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#059669` with a subtle ring of `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`. Error states utilize `#DC2626` with supportive RTL error messages.

### Clinical Checkboxes & Radio Selection
- Recovery kit option selectors feature prominent selectable cards with radio controls anchored on the right.
- Active option triggers a 2px solid `#059669` border with a subtle `#F3F7F5` background shift.

### Sticky Maternal Conversion Drawer (Mobile)
- A persistent bottom drawer that remains unobtrusive during clinical reading.
- Displays the current kit, aggregate OB-GYN rating stars, final price in Egyptian Pounds, and a full-width high-contrast checkout trigger accessible via thumb reach.
